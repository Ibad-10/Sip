What you can do

Sip is a care robot helping an older person drink. Everything it knows comes from its cameras, depth, lidar and what she says.

Talk to it (as her)

  • Water please starts a drink. It finds the glass, pours from the jug, adds a straw and brings it.
  • Ready: the straw only comes to her lips after she says she's ready.
  • That's enough ends the drink. Stop freezes it at once; Go on resumes.

Change the world and watch it adapt

  • Drop chair in hall: lidar sees it and it re-routes.
  • Move the glass: it isn't where remembered, so it searches the rooms.
  • Turn her head: during the approach the arm backs off and waits.
  • Move her to bed: it notices she's gone and looks for her.

In the 3D viewer window

  • Double-click an object to select it, then Ctrl + right-drag to move it, or Ctrl + left-drag to turn it.
  • Space pause, F7 lidar rays, F8 route, F9 camera picture-in-picture.
