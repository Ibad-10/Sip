# Evidence of Need for Each Idea

Gathered on 7 November 2026. Figures are as reported by each source. Market-size estimates come from commercial research firms and vary widely, so use a range in the pitch, never a single number. Re-check any figure against the original page before it goes on a slide.

## 1. Smoke Search (fire scout rover)
| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| 283 fire-related deaths in England, year ending Dec 2025, up from 257 the year before | MHCLG Fire and rescue incident statistics, YE Dec 2025 |
| £12.0 billion: total economic and social cost of fire in England per year (YE Mar 2020) | Home Office, Economic and social cost of fire (2023) |
| 987 firefighters injured at operational incidents in England, YE Mar 2024 (2,408 on-duty injuries in total) | Fire and rescue workforce statistics (via summary) |
| US 2024: 1,388,000 fires, 3,920 civilian deaths, 11,780 civilian injuries | NFPA via NSC Injury Facts |
| US 2025: 83 firefighters died on duty; 53,575 firefighter injuries in 2024, 30% on the fireground | NFPA |
| Firefighting robot market roughly $1.5–3.2 billion in 2025, $2.5–5.7 billion by 2030 (estimates disagree) | Various market reports |
| Fire robots are mostly teleoperated; remote control in complex fires is "problematic", and autonomy is limited | Springer chapter; Frontiers 2024 |

Strength: strong. Official UK government figures, plus a published technology gap.

## 2. Sniffer (gas leak hunter)
| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| US oil and gas methane leaks: $1 billion a year in lost product, $10 billion a year including harm to health and the economy; about 3 times the US government's estimate | Stanford-led study, Nature (2024) |
| "A big part of the cost of reducing leaks can be searching for and locating those leaks" | Harvard Business School summary |
| ICL Plastics, Glasgow, 2004: LPG leak explosion, 9 dead, 33 seriously injured | HSE |
| US gas distribution pipelines, 2010 to Nov 2018: 934 incidents, 92 deaths | FracTracker analysis of PHMSA data |
| Gas detection market roughly $3.8–5.7 billion in 2025 | Various market reports |

Strength: medium. The cost-of-finding-leaks argument is strong; recent UK figures are thin.

## 3. Lost & Found (memory robot for dementia)
| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| About 982,000 people in the UK live with dementia, projected 1.4 million by 2040 | Alzheimer's Society |
| Cost of dementia in the UK £42 billion (2024), projected £90 billion by 2040 | Alzheimer's Society / Carnall Farrar |
| Families bear 63% of the cost of dementia care | Alzheimer's Society |
| How often misplacing items harms daily life: no figure found yet | Gap, search again |

Strength: medium. Large, well-documented population, but no figure yet for the specific problem.

## 4. Rubble Dog (acoustic search in collapsed buildings)
| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| Turkey–Syria earthquake, Feb 2023: over 59,000 deaths; about 6,000 buildings collapsed in Turkey | AP / EBSCO |
| More than 90% of survivors are rescued in the first three days | Ilan Kelman, UCL, quoted by Al Jazeera |
| Rough survival estimate: 74% within 24 hours, 22% after 72 hours, 6% by day five | Steven Godby, Nottingham Trent University (expert estimate, not peer reviewed) |
| Search-and-rescue robot market estimates range from $1.2 billion to $45 billion | Do not use: too inconsistent |

Strength: strong emotionally, and the UCL quote suits a UCL audience. Survival figures are expert estimates, so say so.

## 5. Pill Helper (medication sorting arm)
| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| 237 million medication errors a year in England, 66 million potentially clinically significant | Elliott et al., Sheffield/York (2018) |
| Definitely avoidable adverse drug events cost about £98.5 million a year; press coverage cites £1.6 billion including primary care | Same report; The Independent |
| Adverse drug reactions cause nearly 6% of unplanned hospital admissions in older people | Age UK |
| Errors are more likely in older people and those on many medicines | Same report |
| Caveat: the underlying studies are at least 10 years old | Same report |

Strength: medium-strong, but the data is dated.

## 6. Night Watch (fall finder)
| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| 1 in 3 people over 65, and half of those over 80, fall at least once a year | Public Health England / UKHSA |
| Nearly 1,000 older people a day are admitted to hospital after a fall | Age UK |
| Falls cost the NHS more than £2 billion a year (the £2.3 billion figure is contested) | King's Fund |
| A long lie on the floor raises the risk of pressure sores, dehydration, hypothermia and death | Peer-reviewed literature (PMC) |

Strength: strong need, but a crowded market (see IDEAS.md).

## Effect on the ranking
No change. Smoke Search has the strongest official UK evidence and the clearest published technology gap. Rubble Dog's evidence is the most emotional but the build is hard. Pill Helper's need is real but its data is old.
